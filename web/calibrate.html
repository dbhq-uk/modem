Acoustic calibration

An instrument, not a demo. It measures what this device's microphone actually hears at the four Bell 103 tones, so the two-device mode can be calibrated against real hardware instead of a model.

Run every step on both devices. Steps 1 and 2 are done on each device on its own. Step 3 needs both: one plays, the other listens, then swap.

  1. Room - silence, both devices quiet. 4 seconds.
  2. This device's own speaker - it plays its own mark tone and measures how loud that is in its own microphone. This is the number the whole problem turns on. 4 seconds, and it will be audible.
  3. The other device - put them where you would actually use them. On one device press Play; on the other press Listen at the same time. Then swap roles and do it again.
